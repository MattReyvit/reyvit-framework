# G4 — Operations & Compliance
## Privacy (EU focus, Peru experience)
- GDPR / LOPDGDD: legal basis, privacy notice, data subject rights, retention.
- LSSI-CE: legal notice, cookie consent before non-essential cookies.
- European Accessibility Act + WCAG 2.2.
- EU AI Act: disclose AI-generated interactions where applicable.
- Peru equivalents applied in past projects: Law 29733, consumer complaints book, SUNAT e-invoicing.
## Security & performance
- Lightweight pre-launch security audit (suggested, not automatic).
- Rate limits on public writes; audit log for sensitive actions.
- Monitor errors and latency.
## Leads
- Consent checkbox, source tracking, minimal fields, deletion on request.
